Introduction

Artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming increasingly important in healthcare. Hospitals and healthcare organizations are adopting advanced technologies to improve patient care, manage medical information, and strengthen cybersecurity.

As healthcare systems become more dependent on electronic health records, connected medical devices, and digital communication, protecting sensitive patient information has become a significant concern. Cyberattacks can expose personal medical records, disrupt hospital operations, and threaten patient safety.

Why Healthcare Security Matters

Healthcare organizations collect and store large amounts of personal and medical information. This information must be protected from unauthorized access, data breaches, ransomware attacks, and other cybersecurity threats.

AI technologies can help organizations identify suspicious activity, monitor networks, and respond to potential security threats. However, AI systems also introduce challenges involving privacy, accuracy, and the responsible use of data.

Purpose of This Website

This website examines the relationship between artificial intelligence and healthcare security. It explores the benefits of AI, the cybersecurity challenges healthcare organizations face, and the potential future of AI-powered security systems.
